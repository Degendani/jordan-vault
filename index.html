Before you list

  • The vault price is the lowest StockX ask for a new pair in your size. To sell today you'd take the highest bid, which is lower.
  • For worn pairs, price from GOAT used listings and eBay sold listings. Each pair links to both.
  • Keep the original box, extra laces and hang tags with the pair.
  • Pairs from before about 2012 can have crumbling midsoles. Say so in the listing.
